Who we are

A technology company built for Northeast India's next decade.

Established in 2023 in Guwahati, Assam, PROSMITECH combines software development, AI, Robotics and technology education to solve real problems for governments, institutions, businesses and young people.

01

Company at a glance

Our core strength lies in combining software development, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, digital solutions and technology education to solve real-world problems. We believe technology should not simply digitise an existing process — technology should transform it.

Our objective is to help organisations move from traditional, manual and fragmented systems toward smarter, automated and AI-enabled solutions that improve efficiency, transparency, decision-making and measurable outcomes. At the same time, we're committed to preparing the next generation of Northeast India for a rapidly changing world through AI, Robotics and future-skills education.

Our vision

To build a smarter, future-ready Northeast through technology.

We envision a Northeast India where governments, institutions, businesses and young people are equipped to take advantage of the opportunities created by Artificial Intelligence, automation and emerging technologies — a strong, digitally empowered and future-ready technology ecosystem.
